How this works

Write in the box at the bottom and press the amber button. The two buttons above the box say what your words do: change the image on screen, or make a new one.
The button beside the amber one picks the model and says where it draws. Each idea is a thread that keeps every image made on the way. Command or Control with Enter sends the box.
